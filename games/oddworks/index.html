How to play

Observe. Tinker. Adapt.

  1. Plan freely. Click pulsing places and characters to discover how the market works.
  2. Build a response. Select signs, gates, bells, or carts and place them at junctions. Assign specialists where they help most.
  3. Open the market. Time only counts once you commit. Pause and revise whenever you like.
  4. Find your way. There is no single answer. Try a lean route, a people-first plan, or a grand clockwork contraption.
